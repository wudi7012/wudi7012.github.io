\cvsection{Research}
\publication{Generative Multi-Agent Collaboration in Embodied AI: A Systematic Review, 2025}
\textbf{Accepted by IJCAI 2025, Sole first author.} This work presents the first systematic survey on collaborative behaviors among multiple agents in embodied scenarios from the perspective of the generative-model era. We propose that agents may exist as either embodied entities or internal components within foundation models. This leads to a distinction between external and internal collaboration, which we analyze through key system designs and future directions.

\publication{On-Policy Reinforcement Fine-Tuning with Offline Reward for Multi-Step Embodied Planning, 2025}
\textbf{Accepted by ACL 2026 Main Conference, Sole first author.} This is the first work to apply RLVR-based reinforcement fine-tuning to embodied planning tasks. Unlike conventional reasoning tasks, embodied planning involves a multi-turn, interactive environment. We therefore introduce a simple yet effective offline reward method to address sparse rewards in multi-turn tasks and the high cost of interaction. We provide a theoretical proof of the algorithm\textquotesingle{}s upper bound and achieve SOTA performance on EmbodiedBench. This work has been cited by notable studies such as ERA and Pelican-VL.

\publication{AtomEgo: Exploring Ego--Robot Integration for Embodied Foundation Model Pretraining, 2026}
\textbf{Under review at ICRA 2027; sole first author.} Explores large-scale human egocentric data for embodied foundation model pretraining: (1) builds a unified Ego--Robot dataset of approximately 2,659 hours and a pretraining pipeline; (2) systematically studies three paradigms: joint training with dual action heads, progressive cross-embodiment alignment, and world--action modeling; (3) validates cross-embodiment alignment on real robots, with the best method improving task success from 32.86\% to 53.57\%.

\publication{Leaping Beyond the Teacher via Reinforced On-Policy Distillation for Robotic Manipulation, 2026}
\textbf{Under review at CVPR 2027; co-first author.}
Proposes Reinforced On-Policy Distillation (ReOPD), a VLA post-training framework that integrates dense teacher supervision with sparse environmental rewards. Introduces a stage-adaptive leader--follower mechanism that dynamically shifts optimization priority from teacher guidance to environmental feedback, filtering conflicting learning signals to accelerate early convergence while enabling students to surpass their teachers. The framework supports both discrete-action and flow-matching policies, demonstrating its generality across different VLA architectures. Further extends ReOPD with human-in-the-loop corrections for efficient real-world reinforcement learning, improving task success and generalization across robotic manipulation tasks.

\publication{LaST1.0: A Unified Latent World-Action Model with Control-Centric SpatioTemporal Reasoning, 2026}
\textbf{Under review at ICRA 2027.}
Proposes LaST1.0, a unified latent World-Action Model for fine-grained robotic manipulation. Unlike conventional video-based world modeling, LaST1.0 predicts geometry-aware future latents from wrist-camera observations, focusing on control-relevant robot--object interactions rather than visual reconstruction. Introduces an encoder-free visual architecture and LaST-Attention with 4D-RoPE to preserve fine-grained spatial information and enable structured spatiotemporal reasoning. Future latent prediction serves as training-only supervision, allowing efficient action generation during inference. Achieves state-of-the-art success rates of 92.0\%, 99.0\%, and 86.3\% on RLBench, LIBERO, and LIBERO-Plus, respectively, with strong performance across diverse real-world robotic manipulation tasks.
